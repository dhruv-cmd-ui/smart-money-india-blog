How Much Term Insurance Cover Do You Need?

2026-10-22 · 3 min read

Buying term insurance is one of the most critical financial decisions an Indian investor can make, yet many get stuck on a fundamental question: "How much sum assured is actually enough?" Unlike traditional endowment policies, a term insurance plan is pure life protection designed to replace your financial contribution to your family if you are no longer around. Setting the right coverage amount ensures your dependents can maintain their standard of living and meet long-term goals without compromise.

Calculating Your Cover with the Income-Replacement Method

A practical way to calculate your required cover is the income-replacement method. As a baseline rule of thumb, your sum assured should be at least 10 to 15 times your current gross annual income. However, a comprehensive calculation accounts for three simple factors: replacing future income, paying off liabilities, and funding future family goals, minus your existing liquid assets.

The basic formula is: Required Cover = (Annual Income × Multiplier) + Outstanding Debt + Future Goals - Existing Liquid Assets

Let us walk through a practical rupee example:

Suppose Rahul, age 32, earns an annual income of ₹10 lakh. He has an active home loan balance of ₹30 lakh and wants to secure ₹20 lakh for his child’s higher education. He currently holds ₹5 lakh in mutual funds and fixed deposits.

1. Income replacement (15 × ₹10 lakh) = ₹1.5 crore 2. Add outstanding liabilities = ₹30 lakh 3. Add future goals = ₹20 lakh Total requirement = ₹2 crore 4. Subtract liquid assets = ₹5 lakh

Rahul’s net required cover is ₹1.95 crore (which can be rounded off to a standard ₹2 crore sum assured policy).

Common Underinsurance Mistakes to Avoid

Many Indian policyholders end up underinsured due to a few repeated missteps during their financial planning process.

First, relying entirely on employer group term insurance is a common trap. Corporate policies cease the moment you resign, switch jobs, or face layoffs. Furthermore, group covers are standardized and rarely sufficient to meet a household's actual long-term needs.

Second, picking a random figure like ₹50 lakh or ₹1 crore without doing the math. While ₹1 crore sounds like a substantial sum today, sustained lifestyle inflation weakens its purchasing power over a 20 or 30-year horizon.

Third, mixing protection with investment. Opting for traditional plans or ULIPs instead of pure term insurance often results in paying high premiums for a low sum assured. Pure term plans offer maximum life cover at lower premiums, allowing you to invest your remaining surplus separately in equity or debt instruments managed under SEBI regulations.

When to Review and Upgrade Your Coverage

Your term insurance requirement is not static; it evolves alongside your career and family milestones. A cover calculated in your mid-20s as a single professional will rarely suffice once you take on financial dependents.

You should review your total life cover during major life events: - Marriage: Your financial responsibilities expand to support a spouse. - Parenthood: Raising a child introduces new long-term financial obligations, such as schooling and higher education funds. - Taking large liabilities: Incurring a home loan or high-value business debt requires an immediate increase in cover to ensure the debt does not pass on to your family. - Significant income increases: As your career progresses and your standard of living rises, your replacement income baseline should be adjusted upward.

To accommodate these changes, many insurers offer a life stage upgrade feature, which allows you to increase your sum assured at specific milestones like marriage or childbirth without undergoing fresh medical tests. Alternatively, you can purchase an additional term policy to bridge the protection gap.

Getting the right term insurance cover is not about guessing an arbitrary figure, but systematically evaluating your family's living expenses, liabilities, and future financial goals. By running an income-replacement calculation today, you can eliminate underinsurance and secure your family's financial foundation.
