Retirement Planning in Your 30s: An India-Specific Roadmap

Published 2026-10-26

Walk through NPS, EPF, mutual fund SIPs, and a simple corpus target calculation for someone starting retirement planning in their 30s.

Why This Matters

For most Indian households, decisions like this compound over years, not days — getting the basics right early matters more than optimizing every last detail.

The Practical Approach

Start with the simplest version of the plan that you'll actually stick to, then refine it once it becomes a habit. Complexity that you abandon after a month is worse than a simple plan you follow for years.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

The most frequent mistake isn't picking the 'wrong' option — it's delaying the decision entirely while waiting for a perfect answer that doesn't exist.

Bottom Line

Revisit this decision periodically as your income and goals change, rather than setting it once and never checking back in.
